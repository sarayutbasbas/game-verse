# Perch visual direction

A miniature backyard at dusk, with the living flock as the focus. Twilight #24385a, cornflower #324f72, honey #f6cd79, pale sky #96cbd5, garden green #79b649 and readable paper #fff6da. This game uses blue evening light and sculpted songbirds rather than Draftosaurus's green zoological park. Retain the established Thai body font; a compact, heavy Georgia title gives Perch its own wordmark treatment.

Public display: quiet top toolbar, five-round tracker, current actor, staggered modular garden beside the Fountain, then creatures and player totals. Scale the shared garden to screen height while preserving its column geometry. Phones: private hand first, own flocks on the same geographic board, Fountain and bonus choices, sticky placement confirmation. Five-column maps scroll inside their own board container on narrow phones so the tiles remain legible. Thai action copy is left-aligned; the map, round tracker and private bird selection are centered.

Generated 13 selected sources with built-in image_gen: cover, garden, six-tier Fountain backdrop, five two-pose transparent songbirds, three eight-scene location atlases, a nine-creature atlas and nest/house/lightning atlas. The first four-tier Fountain draft was discarded before integration. Runtime exports contain 49 WebP images (about 4.4 MB): 24 location scenes, 10 bird animation/still files, 9 creatures, 3 objects and 3 main images. The supported first-game setup consumes its verified 13 locations and five creatures; extra generated scenes are not represented as playable unverified content. Exact prompts and paths are recorded in assets.json. scripts/export-perch.mjs exports genuine alpha, aligned sprite baselines, cleaned neighboring fragments, tile crops and optimized WebP files.

Birds switch between actual generated poses. Stacks show up to three representative bodies plus an explicit exact count, with nests beneath and protective houses above. The map has moving evening light; water carries a slow glint. New accepted placements settle into their tile; snapshots/reconnect do not replay placement motion. Local motion preference and prefers-reduced-motion disable every animation. Camera tilt is visual only. All hit regions, labels, scoring, support graph and legal intents are code-owned. Art cannot change a rule.
