# Design direction

White drafting paper #f5f5f0, graphite #202a32, rescue red #d9443f, teal #159ca8, safety yellow #f5cc45, pale blue #d9e9ec. Noto Sans Thai body and bold tabular numerals; no generated text for gameplay values. Reference is the supplied white/red animal-squad comic box, with fresh animal drawings and halftone printing.

Phone: prominent current instruction, own numbered rack, then teammates' anonymous racks. Tap a hidden tile (or two with a detector), choose a held number, confirm question. The addressed teammate answers on their own phone; a successful actor chooses their own matching duplicate without exposing its position beforehand. Shared display: bomb status, validation track, equipment and all anonymous racks, no private values. Every slot retains its position after cuts. Large square touch targets, wire symbols as well as colors, scroll vertically when needed. A single response animation follows accepted state; no artificial time limit.

Review: the rescue-tool workbench and wire racks are the main interface; avoid generic score dashboards. Technical doodles stay at the margins. Comic equipment illustration and bold numbers carry the identity. Prevent global Camel Up wood/header rules from overriding this game's chrome. Respect reduced motion and visible keyboard focus. Use native dialogs and shared restart/exit control.
