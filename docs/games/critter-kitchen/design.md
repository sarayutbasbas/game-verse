# Critter Kitchen — presentation and asset direction

Palette: canal teal #155A61, water #83C5BF, paprika #D85C29, food gold #E7BA25, orchid #9765B4 and plate ivory #FFFDF0. Thai Noto Sans Thai supplies all labels and touch controls. The market itself, ingredients on plates and animal chefs carry the visual identity; thin colored stock borders encode food kinds and queues encode chef capacity. No numbers, costs or effects are baked into generated illustrations.

Shared display: priority hats → critic / six challenges / three rumors → ordered waterfront locations, each with visible stock and capacity1/2/3 queues. Location detail expands on tap. Two-player and standard backgrounds are separate original compositions; location counts and stock differences come from validated boardConfiguration, not the bitmap.

Phone: private actions first, with kitchen and public-board navigation. Full-mobile reveals the same public market beneath private controls; device-local mode changes never send a privileged network action. Chef selection and destination buttons are native touch/keyboard controls. Plans, meal drafts and item previews stay local until confirmation. Accepted server decisions are authoritative. Inventory and image nodes are retained across revision patches; selections clear after a committed pickup, while other players' commitments preserve the local meal or plan draft.

Card detail uses an accessible native modal dialog, full intrinsic image, backdrop blur, body scroll lock, fixed close control and outside dismissal. Final results show a skippable numeric count, winner identity/crown and actual tie-break reason, then remain in a table after local dismissal. Reduced motion disables counting and transitions. No automatic gameplay scroll or toast is introduced.

Original art jobs are recorded in art-jobs.json (95 distinct jobs) plus cover, two board backgrounds and counter texture (4). These are built-in image_gen calls, one per asset. The final manifest records actual files and statuses; source game photos and community-hosted scanned cards are reference-only and are not runtime art. Stable card IDs each map to a distinct original generated illustration. All food values, spice labels, rumor indices, requirements and capacities remain code-rendered.

Audio uses the existing shared Web Audio synthesizer, independent effect/music gains and existing gesture unlock. Accepted sequential public revisions cue pickups, reveal, round refill and endgame; local drafts and hidden commitments do not emit a public gameplay cue. Existing music stays opt-in; no audio service or runtime dependency added.
